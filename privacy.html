Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

What the app is

That's How Publisher is a private tool used only by the team of the YouTube channel That's How (@ThatsHowHQ). Only the channel's own Google accounts sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the consent of the signed-in account, and through the YouTube Data API, the app uploads and updates the channel's own videos: titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails, playlists, and the channel's comments on its own videos. It reads nothing else.

What it keeps

The app stores one access token, encrypted, on a server run by the channel's team. It collects no data about viewers or any other person. It sells, shares and transfers nothing, and uses nothing for advertising. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token is then deleted.
